# Evaluation goal
Decide whether a public Instagram Reel merits further human review for an original Persian content idea. Score between 0 and 10.

# Evidence and rating rubric
- 9–10: Reliable observed exceptional performance against >=3 prior reels from the SAME creator, clear audience tension, strong distinctive content premise, source link and date.
- 7–8: Solid measured outlier ratio or strong multi-source contextual evidence; relevant to Persian-speaking audiences and adaptable without copying.
- 5–6: Promising relatable premise but insufficient trend-growth evidence. Explicitly label as a hypothesis, never a proven trend.
- 3–4: High raw views without comparative evidence or weak topic relevance; standard recycled listicles.
- 0–2: Misleading, unverifiable or potentially harmful; speculative clinical labels, misinformation, privacy violations.

# Mandatory safeguards
A high absolute view count alone does not establish outlier status. If baseline_count < 3 or ratio is missing, say "baseline unavailable". Do not infer private reach, watch time, saves, shares, or algorithms. Distinguish reported public counts, calculated ratios, interpretations, and speculation. Suggest an original perspective rather than copying scripts or claiming causal mechanisms from view counts. Give 3-5 tags.
